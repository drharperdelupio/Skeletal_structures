Drawing Chemical Structures — Interactive Practice

Three parts, all with instant feedback as you go:

  1. Exercise 1 — Complete the Structures: click each carbon to set its hydrogen count, and each non-hydrogen atom to set its lone-pair count.
  2. Exercise 2 — Convert to Skeletal Structures: draw the bond-line (skeletal) structure that matches the structure shown.
  3. Exercise 3 — Convert Skeletal Structures to Expanded Structures: click each vertex or atom to build out the full structure, hydrogens and lone pairs included.

This does not save your progress if you leave the page or refresh — complete it in one sitting.

Exercise 1: Complete the Structures

Each structure below shows the bonds already drawn. Click a carbon to set how many hydrogens complete its octet, or click any other atom to set its lone pairs.

Exercise 2: Convert to Skeletal Structures

Draw the skeletal structure that matches the structure shown, then click Check My Structure.

Exercise 3: Convert Skeletal Structures to Expanded Structures

These are shown in true skeletal notation — carbon vertices are blank until you click them. Click each vertex to set its hydrogen count, and click any labeled heteroatom to set its lone pairs.
